Content
The zipped folder contains the datasets for Balochistan MICS 2019-2020. Nine SPSS data files were produced. Note that data files are only available in SPSS format but can be opened/converted by most recent versions of other statistical software. These files are listed below with the corresponding unit of analysis.
1
hh.sav
Households
- including Water Quality Testing Questionnaire
2
hl.sav
Household members
3
tn.sav
Mosquito nets (in households)
3
wm.sav
Women (age 15-49 years)
4
bh.sav 
Fertility/Birth history module (administered to women age 15-49 years)
5
mm.sav
Maternal mortality module (administered to women age 15-49 years)
6
ch.sav
Children under five
7
fs.sav
Children age 5-17 years
8
mn.sav
Men (age 15-49 years)

Please refer to the Survey Findings Report of Balochistan MICS 2019-2020 for more information on survey and sampling methodology. The standard CSPro CAPI application with data dictionary and standard SPSS syntax files for creation of background variables and tabulations of the Survey Findings Report are available at mics.unicef.org/tools. The customised CAPI application and SPSS syntax for Balochistan MICS 2019-2020 are currently not being distributed.

Please see “Guidelines for merging data files of a MICS survey”, available in the FAQ on mics.unicef.org/faq for further information on the characteristics of the individual files. 

Geocode, i.e., latitude, longitude and altitude, is not available.

Access Conditions
Survey datasets are made publicly available on behalf of the Bureau of Statistics, Planning and Development Department, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa at no cost for legitimate research on condition of:
-	receipt of a description of the objectives of the research project(s),
-	no use of data for purposes other than those expressed in description of the objectives,
-	no redistribution or sharing of data in any form, 
-	citation of data in all reports and publications (see edition notice on the back of the title page of the Survey Findings Reports), and
-	receipt of electronic copies of all reports and publications based on the requested data.

Permission to use is granted through a one-time registration. Each data user must register. If data is used for research objectives other than described during registration, please send an e-mail to mics@unicef.org with updated/additional research objectives.
